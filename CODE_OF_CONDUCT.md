# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TIDE welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on ideas and
code, accept feedback gracefully, and respect privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
trolling, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily
or permanently ban participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
